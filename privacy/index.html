Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the G-Sea Content Creator Bot collects and uses information when you connect your Discord and TikTok accounts.

Information we collect

The service may store:

  • Your Discord user ID
  • Your TikTok username and profile information
  • TikTok follower counts
  • Video views, likes, comments, and shares
  • Statistics for videos related to #Gsea
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens required to connect TikTok

How information is used

This information is used to operate the G-Sea Content Creator program, calculate creator statistics, display progress, create leaderboards, and help G-Sea staff review creator eligibility.

OAuth tokens

TikTok authentication tokens are stored only so the bot can access information authorized by the user. They are not intentionally made public or shared with other creators.

Sharing information

We do not sell personal information. Creator statistics may be displayed to authorized G-Sea staff or through Content Creator program features.

Data retention

Information may remain stored while your account participates in the G-Sea Content Creator program. Data may be removed when it is no longer required to operate the service.

Removing your data

You may request that your connected TikTok account and stored creator information be removed by contacting the G-Sea staff team through the official G-Sea Discord server.

Third-party services

The service interacts with TikTok and Discord. Those services maintain their own privacy policies and data practices.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect stored information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the bot or Content Creator program changes.

Contact

Privacy questions or data removal requests can be sent to the G-Sea staff team through the official G-Sea Discord server.
